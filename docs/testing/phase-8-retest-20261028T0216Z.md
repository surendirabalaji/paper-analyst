## 4. Defects

**P8-D3 (Minor, Frontend 15%: readability of the Routing tab; a regression from the P8-D2 fix).**
- **Repro, no spend:** `.venv/bin/python tests/e2e/phase8_tester_mock_ui.py OUT PROFILE` fails M12b.
- **Repro, deployed:** see `deployed/d1_tab_a_routing.png`.
- **Expected:** short tokens such as "Turn", "#2" and "Agent" stay whole, and only long unbroken tokens wrap.
- **Actual:** "Turn" is split into `T/ur/n`, the `#n` cells into 2 lines, and the Agent column is squeezed to about 50 px.
- **Cause:** `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on every `th, td` drops each cell's min-content width to about 1 character. The table's automatic layout then shrinks the columns whose content is short.
- **Fix direction (developer's choice; verify with M12 and M12b):** scope the wrapping to the cells that can hold long unbroken text.
  - Option 1: put `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on `tr.turn-head td` (the colspan question cell) and on the rationale/inputs and result cells.
  - Option 2: keep it on `td`, but use `overflow-wrap: normal` (or `white-space: nowrap`) for `thead th` and the first `#n` column.
  - Both M12 (columns inside the panel) and M12b must pass.

No other defects found. P8-D1, P8-D2 and N1 are closed.
