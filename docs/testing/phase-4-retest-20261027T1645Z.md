## 4. Evaluation of the pricing ambiguity

- **Documented honestly: yes.** README, architecture §6, `.env.example`, dev_notes and the `policy.py` comment all state three things:
  - the BYOK basis
  - the dated Google two-period price
  - that the higher figure is kept until a Google bill settles the question

  I verified the Google figures independently (R9).
- **Is preferring the reported figure sound? Yes, as a conservative upper bound for budget tracking. But it is not an independent measurement.**
  - OpenRouter's "reported" BYOK cost is OpenRouter's own calculation (tokens × $1.50 / $7.50). It is not Google's invoice.
  - The price table now uses the same rate, so "reported" and "estimated" Gemini figures agree by construction.
  - The label "(reported)" is accurate in that it says *who* computed the figure. It should not be read as "billed".
  - One wording overstates it: `.env.example` says "billed via OpenRouter BYOK = OpenRouter-reported upstream cost".
- **What the student's actual Gemini charge depends on:**
  - **(a) Free tier:** $0 if the BYOK Google key's project is on the Gemini free tier.
  - **(b) Paid tier today:** about half of the figure shown, at $0.75 / $3.75 until 2026-12-31.
  - **(c) Paid tier from 2027-01-01:** equal to the figure shown.

  Phase 9 must present Gemini cost as a labelled, dated estimate, an upper bound, and state the tier. Ideally it should reconcile against the Google Cloud / AI Studio billing view. Recorded as P4-N7.

## 5. Defects and notes

- **P4-D1 (Major): FIXED.** Evidence: R2–R8. Routing decisions are unchanged, and Gemini remains the cheaper side under every listed price.
- **P4-N6 (Low, latent):**
  - `OpenRouterChat._extra` uses `usage.cost` whenever it is non-zero, and only otherwise uses `upstream_inference_cost`.
  - If OpenRouter ever charges a BYOK fee (`cost > 0` together with an upstream cost), only the fee would be counted, and the Google charge would be dropped from `cost_usd`.
  - This does not occur today: R2 shows `cost: 0`.
  - Suggested fix: when `is_byok` is set, use `cost + upstream_inference_cost`.
  - Pinned by strict xfail TC-P4-14, so a fix will flip it to XPASS. Remove the marker then.
- **P4-N7 (Low, documentation, Phase 9):**
  - Mention the Google free-tier possibility next to the BYOK pricing note.
  - Reword `.env.example` from "billed via" to "costed at (OpenRouter-reported upstream rate)".
  - Phase 9 cost tables must label Gemini cost as an estimate and upper bound, state the tier, and reconcile with the Google bill if available.
- **Observation:** `estimate_cost` rounds to $1e-6 per call. This is negligible at current call sizes and is noted only for Phase 9 aggregation.
