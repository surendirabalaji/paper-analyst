## 4. Defects

### P5-D3 (Medium; regression from the P5-D1 fix; RAG citations and Summary "citation preservation")

**What happens.** `synthesize` drops every worker-cited `paper_chunk` whose similarity is below `LOW_RELEVANCE_SCORE` (0.35). That cut-off was calibrated on corpus-wide search: off-topic questions score 0.16–0.26, and in-domain questions score 0.58 or more. Summary's `search_within_paper` is limited to one paper, and the worker writes short keyword queries, so it routinely scores relevant passages from the right paper at 0.20–0.45. Measured with real embeddings:

- Voyager "GPU … fine-tuning" scored 0.30–0.33.
- Self-RAG "limitations" scored 0.23–0.25.

**Effects.**

- Grounded evidence is removed before synthesis.
- The removal is invisible. `done.dropped_citations` lists only markers that appear in the answer, so evidence that synthesis never saw is not reported anywhere.
- The previous run's D4 linked the Experimental Setup chunk to the "gpt-4-0314 APIs" claim. In R5 that detail and its citation are gone.
- Related problem: a chunk's score comes from its first registration. A chunk found first by `search_within_paper` and then read with `get_paper_section` keeps the low score.

**Reproduction.**

- Deployed: `R5_voyager_rerun` in `eval/results/testing/phase5-retest-20261027T1810Z/deployed/`.
- Offline: `tests/unit/test_phase5_tester.py::test_tc_p5_18_in_paper_chunk_from_search_within_paper_is_not_dropped_for_low_global_score`. A Summary task calls `search_within_paper` on 2305.16291, gets an in-paper chunk at 0.29, cites it, and synthesis cites it. The chunk is not linked, and `done` shows `dropped_citations: ['C1']`.

**Expected.**

- A chunk from a paper-scoped Summary tool (outline, section, or within-paper search), cited for a supported claim, reaches synthesis and is linked.
- The off-topic filter stays in place for corpus-wide search results, so TC-P5-01 and TC-P5-02 stay green.
- Any evidence excluded before synthesis is reported in the trace or `done`.
